= How to evaluate a nonprofit software discount
:description: A short checklist before you apply for a vendor nonprofit plan.
:revdate: 2026-08-09

Before you apply:

. Confirm your organization type (often 501(c)(3) or country equivalent).
. Prefer *direct* vendor portals that accept an EIN or determination letter when you want to skip intermediary tokens.
. Capture the offer details (seats, credits, renewal) and a verification date — discounts drift.
. Cross-check https://alternativeto.net/[AlternativeTo] for free and open substitutes.
. Record who in your org owns renewals and compliance.

We will expand this guide as the catalog grows.
